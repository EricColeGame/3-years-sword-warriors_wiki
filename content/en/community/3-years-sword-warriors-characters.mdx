### What are the best ways to upgrade weapons efficiently?

Each star tier significantly multiplies the base damage of your weapon. Reaching 5★ status unlocks the maximum potential of common, rare, and eternity-tier items alike.

### Where can I find specific rare swords?
Rare swords are primarily acquired by hatching world-specific eggs, conquering elite bosses, or purchasing unique variants during active special events and holiday updates.
