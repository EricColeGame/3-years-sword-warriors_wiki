## Frequently Asked Questions

### How is Average DPS calculated for multi-hit abilities?
Average DPS is determined by taking the middle ground between the worst-case scenario (where only a single hit connects with the monster) and the best-case scenario (where every single hit lands successfully).

### What makes 3 years sword warriors skills important for progression?
Understanding **3 years sword warriors skills** allows players to maximize their damage output, choose efficient leveling paths, and select optimal loadouts for both solo farming and group raids.

### Are faster cooldown skills always better than high-damage slow skills?
Not necessarily. While low cooldown abilities offer steady damage, high-damage skills with longer cooldowns are often necessary to burst down elite bosses before they can trigger dangerous phase attacks.

### How can I improve my damage consistency against moving targets?
Focus on abilities with reliable hit boxes or high single-hit percentages, and practice timing your strikes to coincide with enemy recovery frames or crowd-control stuns.
