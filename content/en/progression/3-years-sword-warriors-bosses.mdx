Runes play a critical role in determining your overall effectiveness against elite foes. To begin utilizing runes, adventurers must first open Rune Chests or acquire them directly through the Rune Shop. Once obtained, these powerful artifacts grant essential rune power.

Rune power directly dictates how much bonus damage you can deal to major targets while simultaneously reducing the incoming damage your character absorbs. 

| Rune Level Milestone | Stat Bonus Effect | Resource Cost Focus |

As player experience shows, maximizing your runes up to level 16 requires a steady accumulation of Gems. Dedicating time to farm necessary upgrade materials will drastically improve your survivability during extended combat phases.
