## Hero Management and Farming Roles

Heroes play a pivotal role in maintaining high clear speeds. Setting up dedicated heroes for specific tasks—such as gem farming or experience gathering—allows you to maximize your resource gain over time.

### Key Hero Roles for Progression

*   **The EXP Farmer:** Specialized heroes equipped with experience-boosting passive traits or ornaments to speed up your leveling curve.
*   **The Gem Farmer:** Heroes optimized for rapid mob clearing to maximize currency generation for upgrades and summons.
*   **Titan Heroes:** High-tier powerhouses acquired via the Crown Store or special events that feature massive area-of-effect (AoE) or splash damage capabilities.

Managing your active hero bonuses effectively during combat will prevent bottlenecks when tackling tougher boss encounters in higher worlds.

## Advanced Strategies: Rebirths, Runes, and Endless Mode

Once you reach the mid-to-endgame threshold, standard leveling methods begin to slow down. At this stage, you need to leverage advanced game systems to keep your progression moving forward.

### Rebirth Packs and Stat Resets
Utilizing high-tier rebirth packs (such as the 80 or 100 Rebirth Packs) gives seasoned players the multiplicative boosts needed to break through defensive walls. Plan your rebirths around double-EXP events or daily playtime rewards to maximize efficiency.

### Rune Systems and Skill Points
Unlocking the Rune Shop introduces specialized passive modifiers that can completely alter your build. Furthermore, acquiring Skill Points from modes like the Eternal Guardian Hall in Endless Mode lets you tailor your combat prowess for high-level survival.

### Daily Routines for Maximum Efficiency
*   **Claim Daily Rewards:** Take advantage of limited-time daily hero claims and login events.
*   **Complete Daily Missions:** Ensure you knock out all routine quests for a steady stream of secondary currencies.
*   **Participate in Limited Events:** Seasonal invasions and boss events often provide exclusive progression materials unavailable elsewhere.
