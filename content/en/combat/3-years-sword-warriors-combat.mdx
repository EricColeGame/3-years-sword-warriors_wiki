## Advanced Tactical Maneuvers for Competitive Play

Surviving high-tier skirmishes demands mastery over advanced movement and defensive options. Dodging provides essential invincibility frames (i-frames), but misusing it leaves you vulnerable to punishing counterattacks. 

*   **Timing Your Parries:** Intercepting an incoming strike at the exact moment of impact nullifies damage and opens the opponent up for a critical counter-strike.
*   **Stamina Conservation:** Never drain your meter completely; always leave enough energy to execute an emergency evasion or block.
*   **Spacing and Footwork:** Maintaining optimal distance prevents enemies from landing sweet-spot hits while allowing your longer-range attacks to connect.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes 3 years sword warriors combat different from earlier versions?

### How can I improve my parrying consistency?
Practice against AI or training dummies to learn enemy attack wind-ups. Focus on the weapon flash or sound cue rather than the overall animation to time your blocks accurately.

Yes, while slower, heavy weapons possess incredible stagger potential and guard-breaking capabilities that allow solo players to control crowd engagement effectively.
