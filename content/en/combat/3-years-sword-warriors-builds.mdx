## Optimizing Your Build Strategy

Creating a truly powerful combat setup involves more than just equipping the weapon with the highest raw damage number. Successful players balance several core pillars:

- **Resource Management:** Balance your spending between hatching world eggs and upgrading your current weapon's star levels. Upgrading a rare weapon often yields better results than gambling on low-chance drops.
- **Endless Mode Scaling:** Keep in mind that certain weapons provide passive percentage boosts to damage in endless mode (+10% scaling up to +20% at higher tiers). Factor this in if your primary focus is wave survival.
- **Event Participation:** Limited-time events frequently reward powerful eternity-rarity items that outclass standard world drops. Always check active event requirements to secure free high-tier gear.
- **Ornament Synergies:** Pair your active sword choice with complementary hero ornaments to maximize your stat bonuses across critical hits and attack speed.
